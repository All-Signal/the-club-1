# 🗂️ VAULT ARCHITECTURE & TAXONOMY

This Obsidian Vault is engineered as an interconnected associative network. It rejects deep, siloed folders in favor of wide interlinking and Map of Content (MOC) index hubs.

---

## Folder Map & Taxonomy

```
the-club-1/
├── 00 - Meta & Manifesto/              -> Core philosophy, governance, foundational doctrines
├── 01 - Divergent Personas/            -> Deep dossiers on target archetypes (18-25)
├── 02 - Cross-Domain Verticals/        -> 22 cross-disciplinary domain collision nodes
├── 03 - Acquisition & Outreach Playbooks/ -> Tactical channel playbooks & infiltration tactics
├── 04 - Operations & Vetting/          -> Gauntlet, crucible screening, collision protocols
├── 05 - Canvas & Graph/                -> Obsidian visual canvas (.canvas) diagrams
├── Templates/                          -> Standardized markdown schemas for new nodes
└── attachments/                        -> High-resolution images and field notes
```

---

## Metadata Frontmatter Standards

Every note in this vault should carry structured YAML frontmatter:

```yaml
---
title: "Exact Title"
aliases: ["Alias 1", "Alias 2"]
tags: ["#tag1", "#tag2"]
persona: "Target Persona (if applicable)"
vertical: "Vertical Domain (if applicable)"
status: "active | draft | archived"
clearance: "Public | Tier-1 | Tier-0"
last_updated: "YYYY-MM-DD"
---
```

## Color-Coded Graph Visualization

The vault is configured with `.obsidian/graph.json` color groupings:
- 🔴 **`#meta` / `#governance`** (Red/Pink): Structural and authority nodes
- 🔵 **`#persona`** (Cyan): Target divergent personas
- 🟣 **`#vertical`** (Purple): Cross-disciplinary vertical domains
- 🟠 **`#playbook`** (Orange): Channel acquisition playbooks
